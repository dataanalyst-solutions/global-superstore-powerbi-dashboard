# Global Superstore Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard

Power BI | DAX | Power Query | Data Analytics | Data Visualization

## Project Overview

An interactive 4-page Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Global Superstore sales, profitability, customers, products, segments and markets.

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 — Executive Sales Dashboard

Provides an overall view of sales, profit, customers, orders, products, markets, regions and categories.

### Page 2 — Customer & Products Analysis

Focuses on customer rankings, product performance, segment analysis and market performance.

### Page 3 — Sales & Profit Analysis

Analyzes monthly sales and profit trends along with category-level sales and profitability.

### Page 4 — Geographic & Market Analysis

Analyzes sales and profitability across geographic regions and markets to identify regional performance and business opportunities.
