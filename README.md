# Global Superstore Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard

**Power BI | DAX | Power Query | Data Modeling | Data Analytics | Data Visualization**

## Project Overview

An interactive **4-page Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze Global Superstore sales, profitability, customers, products, segments, markets and geographic performance.

The dashboard transforms transactional data into interactive business insights through KPI tracking, trend analysis, customer and product analysis, category performance and geographic analysis.

## Key Performance Indicators

* Total Sales: 13M
* Total Profit: 1.47M
* Profit Margin: 12%
* Average Order Value: 505.01
* Total Customers: 5K
* Total Orders: 25K
* Total Products: 10K

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 — Executive Sales Dashboard

Provides an overall view of sales, profit, customers, orders, products, markets, regions and categories.

### Page 2 — Customer & Products Analysis

Focuses on customer rankings, product performance, segment analysis and market performance.

### Page 3 — Sales & Profit Analysis

Analyzes monthly sales and profit trends along with category-level sales and profitability.

### Page 4 — Geographic & Market Analysis

Analyzes sales and profitability across geographic regions and markets to identify regional performance and business opportunities.
